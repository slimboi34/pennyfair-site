Frequently asked questions

How do I split by item?

Add everyone under People, then tap an item and tick who had it. If someone had more of a shared item, give them extra portions. Items nobody is ticked on are shared by everyone.

How are tip and tax shared?

In proportion to what each person ordered. If you had half the food, you pay half the tip. You can turn on Tip on total after tax if that's the custom where you are.

Why does one person pay a penny more?

Some totals can't be divided exactly, like £10 between three people. Pennyfair works in whole pennies so the shares always add up to the exact bill, and hands the leftover pennies to different people in turn.

The receipt scan missed an item or misread a price.

You always get a review screen before anything is added: untick lines that aren't items and fix any name or price. Scanning works best with the receipt flat, in good light, with the whole width in view. You can also paste the receipt's text, or add items by hand.

Should I add tax?

Only if it's added at the till (like US sales tax). Where prices already include it (like UK VAT), leave tax at 0.

Where is my data?
