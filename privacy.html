Pennyfair

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Pennyfair splits bills on your iPhone. It has no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) do not receive any of your data.

What stays on your phone

The bills you create (names of people, items, prices, tip, tax and who has paid), your saved History and your settings (default currency, tip, tax and your name) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are included in your normal iPhone backups under Apple's control. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete all saved bills, removes them.

Camera and photos

If you choose Scan with camera, iOS asks for permission to use the camera. The camera is used only to capture the receipt you are scanning. If you choose Choose a photo, the iOS photo picker gives the app only the one photo you pick; Pennyfair cannot see the rest of your library. In both cases, the text is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition (the Vision framework). Receipt images are not saved by the app and are never uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share, iOS shows its standard share sheet. Only the summary text you see is shared, and only with the apps and people you pick.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No network requests: the app works fully offline.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because we never receive it.

Children

Pennyfair is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
